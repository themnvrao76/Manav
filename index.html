HierarchicalNets

Learning the relationships between poses.

Hierarchical vision transformers use coarse-to-fine supervision to distinguish visually similar yoga poses across a three-level taxonomy. Four architectures explore the trade-off between model capacity and fine-grained recognition.

93.07%Top-1 accuracy at the 82-class level on Yoga-82

INT. JOURNAL OF ADVANCED MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGY · 2026

PhyViT-GAN

Generating images from physical trajectories.

A physics-guided generative model translates simulated component-displacement trajectories into future-state inspection images. A trajectory encoder and VAE-GAN connect numerical simulation with visual prediction.

0.8348Structural similarity (SSIM) on held-out data
