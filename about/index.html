About

I like problems where vision meets reasoning.

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Systems Science at the State University of New York at Binghamton, working at the intersection of multimodal AI, computer vision, and human-centered visual understanding.

What I work on

My research centers on vision-language models and geometry-aware reasoning. I’m interested in models that can connect what they say to what they actually observe — especially for structured visual domains such as human pose.

My background also includes computer vision engineering, medical-image segmentation, fine-grained classification, deep-learning systems, and applied research.
